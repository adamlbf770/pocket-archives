# Pocket Archives landing-page image sources

These images are presented editorially to identify artists and visual traditions represented by Pocket Archives.

- `pokemon-mystery-dungeon-sugimori-manga.jpg` — page from the 16-page *Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time* manga written and illustrated by Ken Sugimori. Attribution record: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon%3A_Explorers_of_Time_and_Darkness_(manga). Scan source: https://www.tumblr.com/beetlejump/125985359678/this-is-a-16-page-full-color-comic-that-came-in
- `pokemon-adventures-rgb-volume-1.jpg` — cover of *Pokémon Adventures (Red and Blue), Vol. 1*. Story by Hidenori Kusaka; art by Mato. Official publisher record and image: https://www.viz.com/manga-books/manga/pokemon-adventures-volume-1-0/product/1958
- `pokemon-rgb-sugimori-watercolor.jpg` — user-supplied image of early Red, Green, and Blue-era Pokémon watercolor artwork featuring Red, Blue, Pikachu, and Charizard; attributed to Ken Sugimori.
- `mtg-shivan-dragon-alpha.jpg` — *Shivan Dragon* by Melissa A. Benson, *Magic: The Gathering* Limited Edition Alpha (1993). Card record and image service: Scryfall: https://scryfall.com/card/lea/174/shivan-dragon
